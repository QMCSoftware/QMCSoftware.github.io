Publications

Publication and citation resources for QMCSoftware projects.

This page collects papers, reports, and theses related to QMCSoftware and quasi-Monte Carlo methods. Each item includes a short description and links to publication records or accessible preprints.

Featured introduction

Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods: What, Why, and How?

Fred J. Hickernell, Nathan Kirk, and Aleksei G. Sorokin

Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo 2024, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics, volume 522, pages 3–40, 2026.

DOI Springer

This paper is a tutorial on what quasi-Monte Carlo is, why it can improve upon ordinary Monte Carlo, and how problems can be formulated to benefit from QMC.

Publication catalog

Papers and reports related to QMCSoftware and quasi-Monte Carlo methods, ordered by publication year, newest first.

Fast Bayesian multilevel quasi-Monte Carlo

Aleksei G. Sorokin, Pieterjan Robbe, Gianluca Geraci, Michael S. Eldred, Fred J. Hickernell

Journal of Computational Physics, volume 569, article 115517, 2027.

DOI Publisher

Combines Bayesian cubature with multilevel quasi-Monte Carlo, using Gaussian process posterior uncertainty to guide adaptive sample allocation.

Empirical Bernstein and betting confidence intervals for randomized quasi-Monte Carlo

Aadit Jain, Fred J. Hickernell, Art B. Owen, Aleksei G. Sorokin

Information and Inference: A Journal of the IMA, volume 15, issue 1, article iaag003, 2026.

DOI Publisher

Investigates empirical Bernstein and hedged betting confidence intervals for randomized quasi-Monte Carlo estimates of bounded integrands.

Enhancing Neural Autoregressive Distribution Estimators for Image Reconstruction

Ambrose Emmett-Iwaniw, Nathan Kirk

Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo 2024, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics, volume 522, pages 259–275, 2026.

DOI Publisher

Studies autoregressive image reconstruction from observed pixel subsets, including pixel designs inspired by low-discrepancy sampling.

Fast multitask Gaussian processes, with application to surrogate modeling of the quark-gluon plasma

Aleksei G. Sorokin, Pieterjan Robbe, Yen-Chun Liu, Simon Mak, Fred J. Hickernell

arXiv preprint arXiv:2603.16014, 2026.

arXiv preprint

Combines low-discrepancy designs with structured kernels to accelerate exact multitask Gaussian process fitting, with applications to surrogate modeling of the quark-gluon plasma.

High-dimensional quasi-Monte Carlo via combinatorial discrepancy

Jiaheng Chen, Haotian Jiang, Nathan Kirk

Journal of Complexity, volume 96, article 102053, 2026.

DOI Publisher

Constructs high-dimensional quasi-Monte Carlo point sets using combinatorial discrepancy, with error analysis for weighted function spaces and low effective dimension.

Multilevel Sampling in Algebraic Statistics

Nathan Kirk, Ivan Gvozdanović, Sonja Petrović

Algebraic Statistics (forthcoming, special issue for Henry Wynn), 2026.

Publication status arXiv preprint

Adapts multilevel sampling ideas to lattice fibers in algebraic statistics and evaluates sample quality with coverage and discrepancy measures.

Neural Low-Discrepancy Sequences

Michael Etienne Van Huffel, Nathan Kirk, Makram Chahine, Daniela Rus, T. Konstantin Rusch

Proceedings of the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, volume 306, pages 123416–123433, 2026.

Publisher

Uses neural networks to construct finite low-discrepancy sequences by learning index-to-point maps and reducing discrepancies across sequence prefixes.

Optimal Monte Carlo integration on matrix manifolds

Aleksei G. Sorokin, Yihan Guo, Lek-Heng Lim

arXiv preprint arXiv:2610.16965, 2026.

arXiv preprint

Develops optimally weighted Monte Carlo integration rules on matrix manifolds using computable Sobolev kernels. The weights avoid kernel mean embedding calculations, and numerical experiments include an application to intrinsic volumes.

Aleksei G. Sorokin

Publisher
